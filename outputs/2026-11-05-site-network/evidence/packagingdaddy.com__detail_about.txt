Our Story
Founded in 2011, PACKAGING DADDY (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED started as a small print shop with a big dream — to revolutionize the packaging industry with quality, innovation, and sustainability at its core.
What began as a two-person operation has grown into one of the most trusted packaging companies globally, serving over 5,000 businesses across 20+ industries. From startups to Fortune 500 companies, we've been the packaging partner of choice for brands that refuse to compromise.
Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility spans 50,000 sq ft and houses the latest in digital, offset, and flexographic printing technology, enabling us to handle orders of any scale with unmatched precision and speed.
