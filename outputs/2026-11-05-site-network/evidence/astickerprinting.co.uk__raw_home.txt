Frequently asked questions
How long does sticker printing take?
Typical turnaround is 10–12 working days, depending on the product, quantity and customisation. The time starts from the day you approve your artwork and digital proof. If you need faster delivery, contact us before you order.
Will I see a proof before my stickers are printed?
Yes. We send a PDF proof for approval, so you can check the design before printing. Production goes ahead exactly as approved, so review the proof carefully.
What artwork files should I send?
Supply artwork in CMYK at a minimum of 300 DPI, with 3mm of bleed on every side. We accept .cdr and .eps files in CMYK layers, and you can send a PDF for a quick look first.
Can you print stickers in a custom size or shape?
Yes. Give us the finished size in millimetres. For die-cut shapes, supply the design with the cutting line drawn as a 1mm black line on a white background.
Do you deliver across the UK?
Yes, we deliver UK-wide. We aim to ship orders straight after they are printed.
