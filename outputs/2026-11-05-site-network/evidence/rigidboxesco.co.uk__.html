Five Simple Steps

Putting a Rigid Boxes Co gift box together could not be easier.

  1. Step 1 Supplied flat for minimal storage.
  2. Step 2 Remove the corner papers to reveal the sticky surface.
  3. Step 3 Pinch the corners firmly together.
  4. Step 4 Concealed magnetic snap shut closure.
  5. Step 5 Tie a bow, or choose a box without ribbon.

Questions

How are the boxes delivered?

Flat, in individual clear bags, packed in cartons of 12.

How do I put a box together?

Remove the corner papers, pinch the corners and the box holds its shape. It takes seconds.

Can you print my logo?

Yes. 24 boxes per size is the minimum, and 144 or more is the most economical quantity.

Can you make a box to my own design?

Yes, on a quantity of 2,000 or more, with a lead time of 12 to 16 weeks.
