Questions

Asked before most orders

+ What size should I order?
Measure the product, allow a little for a hand going in, and send width × gusset × height in millimetres. If you would rather not measure, send a photograph of what you are packing and we will specify it.
+ Can you print on brown kraft and white stock?
Both, smooth or ribbed. Brown kraft is ideal for one and two colour work; white or coated board is the one for photographic print.
+ Do you supply samples?
Yes. Tell us which bags you want to see and where to send them.
+ What artwork do you need?
Vector is best — .cdr, .eps or a print-ready PDF in CMYK. A logo file or a sketch is enough to begin a quote.
+ Which finishes are available?
Matt and gloss lamination, foil blocking, embossing and debossing, eyelets, and a choice of twisted paper, rope, ribbon or cotton handles.
+ Do you deliver outside Sydney?
Yes, anywhere in Australia. Delivery is confirmed with your quote.
