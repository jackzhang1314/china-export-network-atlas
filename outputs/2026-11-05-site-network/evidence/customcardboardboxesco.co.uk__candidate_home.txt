Frequently Asked Questions
What is your return policy?
If something isn’t right with your order, we’ve got you covered. We accept returns and offer free reprints for any printing errors or damaged boxes. Your satisfaction always comes first.
How long does delivery take?
Most custom box orders are printed and delivered within 3–5 working days across the UK. For bulk or international orders, it may take up to 10–14 days depending on the courier and order size.
Do you provide worldwide delivery?
Yes, we ship globally. Whether you’re in the UK or overseas, we ensure quick and secure delivery of your custom boxes right to your doorstep.
How do I track my shipment?
Once your order is dispatched, you’ll receive an email with a live tracking link so you can follow your delivery every step of the way.
