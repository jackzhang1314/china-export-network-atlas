Frequently Asked Questions
What types of custom hang tags do you offer?
We offer swing tags, folded tags, pricing tags, product info tags, and luxury foil-finished tags. You can choose any size, shape, material, or finish to match your brand style.
Which materials can I choose for my hang tags?
Our hang tags are printed on premium card stock, Kraft paper, textured paper, matte or glossy finishes, and eco-friendly recycled materials. Each option is durable and suitable for apparel, accessories, gifts, and retail products.
Can I print my logo, barcode, or product details on the tags?
Yes, we can print logos, QR codes, product descriptions, barcodes, care instructions, pricing, and custom artwork to fully brand your hang tags.
How fast can I receive my custom hang tags?
Most orders are printed and shipped within a few business days. Fast-turnaround options are available for urgent orders or product launches.
Do you offer eco-friendly hang tags?
Yes, our hang tags can be made from recycled and sustainable materials and printed with eco-friendly inks to keep your branding premium and environmentally responsible.
