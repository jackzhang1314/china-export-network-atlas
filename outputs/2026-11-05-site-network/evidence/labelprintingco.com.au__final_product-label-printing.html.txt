Product Labels
Product labels
are simple labels which are affixed on all types and categories of products. These products may range from daily consumption items, capital good, industrial products, services and many others. Any type of details can be printed on them, such as, brand, company and country of make, manufacturing/expiry dates, operating/safety instruction, ingredients or any other legally required details.
Labelprintingco.com.au offers six different types of labels for products which are; round, Die-Cut, Oval, rectangular and custom labels. All these designs can be printed in any color, any size and any shape, as required by your marketing plan and marketing mix for the specific product.
Vast variety of the materials is available for the printing of the products labels, such as, all qualities of paper, soft / hard cardboard, UV coated material, clear/transparent/white Vinyl plastic, PVC plastic and metal / metal foiled material.
Images and text are printed with different combinations of full, bright, shining and eye-catching colors while some labels are printed with CMYK colors. Some of the labels are printed, using the embossing and stamping techniques while the Gold and Silver foiled materials are also used for the printing of high-priced and luxury products.
