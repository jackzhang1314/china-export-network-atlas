Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q: Why are stickers a good promotional product?
    A: They are a low cost and creative way to promote your brand or business.
  • Q: Do you have a minimum order quantity requirement?
    A: There is no MOQ, you can order as little as 1pc.
  • Q: How long do your stickers usually last?
    A: Our stickers are fade resistant, and usually come with an extra layer of UV protection, but the product’s longevity depends on weather, storage, or exposure. Typically they last for 1-3 years.
  • Q: What are the minimum and maximum sizes you can print?
    A: We have no minimum or maximum requirements. If you need any help with sizing, our design team will happily give suggestions.
  • Q: What are the most popular stickers for business usage?
    A: The most popular are die-cut stickers, made to fit around your logo. There are also wall graphics, name tags, and custom labels.
  • Q: Can I add a scannable barcode or QR code to my sticker?
    A: Yes, we can print fully functional barcodes or QR codes!
  • Q: Do you offer online design services?
    A: Yes, of course! Welcome to use our self-developed online design system anytime, anywhere. You can upload your own artwork to make stickers design, and you can also make your own stickers by using our exquisite design templates & elements to add text, logo, etc. If there is any question about your own design, our professional designers will help you with enthusiasm. Most importantly, these are all free at VinylStickers.Com!
  • Q: Does the cutting line need to display in the artwork?
    A: No, we will add a cutting line to your artwork and provide it to you for confirmation.
  • Q: Will I receive the proof of the final design after ordering?
    A: On this issue, we provide customers with two choices, one is that you ask to confirm design artwork, the other is that free validation. If you only customize the regular vinyl stickers and have no special requirements, you can arrange the production directly without choosing to confirm the artwork, so that you will receive your products quickly.
  • Q: What are the shipping details?
    A: We offer free shipping services to USA / Canada / Europe. After confirming the design drawings, the goods can be delivered within 1-3 days. However, due to the place of receipt, the number of orders, and other factors, the delivery time may vary.
  • Q: After approving the artwork, can I make changes to the artwork?
    A: Yes, you can. If your stickers online order has been produced, we will charge part of the fee according to the production tempo. If it has not been produced, there will be no fee for modification.
  • Q: When I am not satisfied with the product, can you accept the return?
    A: We only accept refunds for quality problems, wrong products, and transportation damage.
  • Q: Can I put your custom stickers on my skin surface?
    A: Usually, if you are not sensitive skin, you can paste cheap stickers on the skin, but pay attention to staying in the skin vision for no more than 6 hours.
