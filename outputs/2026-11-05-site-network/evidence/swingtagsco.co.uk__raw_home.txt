Frequently Asked Questions
What types of swing tags do you print?
Swing tags, clothing and apparel tags, gift tags, bottle neck tags, luggage tags, fold-over tags, jewellery tags and custom die-cut shapes. If you need a shape that is not on the site, send the outline and we cut to it.
What is the minimum order?
One hundred tags, per design. If you need two different tags, each is its own run of 100.
How much do custom swing tags cost?
Pricing starts at £0.02 per tag. What you pay depends on the stock, the size, how many colours you print and which finishes you add — foiling and embossing cost more than plain printing. Send the specification and the quote comes back itemised.
Which materials can I choose?
Recycled kraft, art paper, premium card, leather, waterproof PVC and silkscreen fabric. Tell us how the tag will be handled and we will recommend a stock.
Can you cut a shape of my own?
Yes. Send the outline you want and it is produced as a custom die-cut, in any size.
Will I see a proof before it is printed?
Yes. Our design team creates a digital proof from your requirements, and nothing goes on press until you approve it.
How long does printing and delivery take?
Ten to twelve working days from artwork approval, then delivery across the UK.
