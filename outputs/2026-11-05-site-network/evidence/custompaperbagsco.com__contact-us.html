What to include in an inquiry

  • Bag type, or what you are packing
  • Size in inches - width x gusset x height - if you know it
  • Quantity, and whether it repeats
  • Artwork or a logo file
  • Your deadline

What happens next

Your inquiry is logged and reaches the sales inbox with the artwork attached. We reply with a price for your exact job, and if the deadline is tight we will say so rather than let it run late.

Frequently asked questions

+ What are your opening hours?
Mon-Fri, 9am-6pm US Eastern.
+ Can I send artwork after the inquiry?
Yes. The quote can start from a description; the print file cannot start until the artwork is in.
+ What do you need to give me a price?
Bag type, size, quantity and artwork, or just tell us what you are packing and we will suggest the rest.
