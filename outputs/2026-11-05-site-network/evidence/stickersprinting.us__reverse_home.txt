Frequently Asked Questions
What is your return policy?
If something isn’t right with your order, we’ve got you covered. We accept returns and offer free reprints for any printing errors or damaged labels. Your satisfaction always comes first.
How long does delivery take?
Most custom label orders are printed and delivered within 3–5 working days across the UK. For bulk or international orders, it may take up to 10–14 days depending on the courier and order size.
Do you provide worldwide delivery?
Yes, we ship globally. Whether you’re in the UK or overseas, Label Printing Co. ensures quick and secure shipping right to your doorstep.
How do I track my shipment?
Once your order is dispatched, you’ll receive an email with a live tracking link so you can follow your delivery at every step until it arrives.
