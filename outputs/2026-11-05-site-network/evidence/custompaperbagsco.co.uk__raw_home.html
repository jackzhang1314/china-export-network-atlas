Kraft paper bags

Unbleached brown kraft, 180-250gsm. The everyday British carrier: strong, cheap to print, recyclable with the paper.

Questions

Asked before most orders

+ What size should I order?
Measure the product, add a little for a hand going in, and send width × gusset × height in millimetres. If you would rather not measure, send a photograph of what you are packing and we will specify it.
+ Do you print on brown kraft and white?
Both, smooth or ribbed. Brown kraft takes one and two colour work beautifully; white or coated board is the one to use for photographic print.
+ Can I have a sample before ordering?
Yes, samples are free. Say which bags you want to see and where to send them.
+ What artwork do you need?
Vector is best: .cdr, .eps or a print-ready PDF in CMYK. A logo file or even a sketch is enough to start the quote.
+ Are the bags recyclable?
Uncoated paper and board go in kerbside recycling. A laminated bag has a film over the board, so it depends on local facilities - ask us for the stock specification on your job and we will tell you what is on it.
+ Do you deliver outside London?
Yes, anywhere in the UK. Delivery is confirmed with your quote.
