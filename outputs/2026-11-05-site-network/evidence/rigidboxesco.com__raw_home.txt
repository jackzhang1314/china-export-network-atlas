What are collapsible rigid boxes?
A collapsible rigid box is a rigid box with hinged walls. It is made from the same thick paperboard as a standard rigid box and wrapped in printed or specialty paper, so it has the same weight and finish. The difference is in how it travels. A standard rigid box is delivered fully formed and takes up its full volume in a carton and on a shelf. A collapsible box is delivered folded flat and is assembled by hand in a few seconds, usually with a magnetic flap that holds the lid shut.
Brands use collapsible rigid boxes for cosmetics, jewelry, apparel, candles, electronics and gift sets, and for PR mailers and subscription orders where presentation matters as much as protection.
Frequently asked
questions
What is a collapsible rigid box?
A rigid box with hinged walls. It folds flat for shipping and storage and is assembled by hand when needed.
Is it as strong as a standard rigid box?
It is made from the same thick board. Once assembled, the walls lock in place and the box holds its shape.
How long does one box take to assemble?
A few seconds. Lift the walls, press the corners into place and close the lid.
Can you print inside the box?
Yes. The inside of the lid and walls can be printed, and inserts can be made to hold your product.
Can I order a custom size?
Yes. Every box is made to order around your product's dimensions.
What is the minimum order?
The minimum order is 50 boxes, and prices start from $1 per box. The unit price falls as the quantity rises.
