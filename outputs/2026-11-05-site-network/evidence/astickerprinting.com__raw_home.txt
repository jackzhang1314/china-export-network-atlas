How Ordering Custom Stickers Works With Us
Straight answers to the questions people ask before their first order, and what you can expect from us.
How do I order custom stickers?
Request a quote with the size, shape, quantity and material you want, and attach your artwork if you have it. We review the file, email you a PDF proof, and start printing once you approve it.
What file format should my sticker artwork be?
Send .cdr or .eps files in CMYK at 300 dpi, with 3mm of bleed on every side. A PDF is fine for a first look, and if you do not have print-ready files our team can help recreate the design.
Will I see a proof before my stickers are printed?
Yes. We email a PDF proof and wait for you to confirm it is approved for printing. Checking the proof carefully avoids errors, and your timeline starts from the day you approve it.
How much do custom stickers cost?
Price depends on size, shape, material, finish and quantity, so every order is quoted individually. Share those details on the quote form and we will price your exact job.
Can you print stickers in a custom shape?
Yes. Sizes and shapes are made to your dimensions. For die cut stickers, mark the cutting line on your artwork with a 1mm black line so we can follow the outline you want.
Can I get my stickers faster?
Timing depends on the product, quantity and customization. If you need faster delivery, contact us before you place the order so we can confirm what is possible.
