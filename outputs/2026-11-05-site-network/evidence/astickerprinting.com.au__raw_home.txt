Why order here
How ordering custom stickers works
A quote-led process: you tell us what you need, check a proof, and only then do we print.
How do I get a price for my stickers?
Use the custom quotation form. Include the size, shape, material if you know it, quantity and your artwork. Prices depend on those details, so a quote is the accurate way to price your order.
What happens after I send my artwork?
We prepare a PDF proof and email it to you. Check the wording, layout and colours, then confirm it is approved for printing. Production starts from the day you approve the proof.
Can you design the sticker for me?
Yes. Design support is free. Send a logo, a photo or a rough idea and we will set up the artwork with you before the proof stage.
Is there a charge for dies or printing plates?
No. Die and plate set-up is not charged separately, including for die cut shapes made to the outline of your design.
Can a rush order be arranged?
If you have a deadline, contact us before you order so we can tell you what is possible for your job. Delays in approving artwork move the delivery date, so approving your proof promptly helps.
