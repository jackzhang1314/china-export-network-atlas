Frequently asked
questions
Quick answers so you can brief a job without a phone call.
+
What is the minimum order?
Minimum order depends on the bag style and size. Tell us the bag and the quantity you have in mind and we will confirm it with your price.
+
How much do custom paper bags cost?
Price depends on the size, the quantity, the stock and how many colors are printed. We confirm your price and delivery date before you pay.
+
How long does an order take?
Lead time depends on the run and the finish, counted from the day you approve the proof. Tell us the deadline with your inquiry and we will say whether it fits.
+
Can I order a size that is not listed?
Yes. Bags are made to your dimensions. Send width, gusset and height in inches, and say what you are packing so we can match the stock weight.
+
What artwork do you need?
Vector is best: .cdr, .eps or a print-ready PDF in CMYK. If you only have a logo image or a sketch, send it: our team sets the file up for you at no charge.
+
Can I see a sample first?
Yes, free samples are available. Ask with your inquiry and say which bags you want to see.
+
What paper do you print on?
Kraft, coated and laminated paper and card. Stock weight is matched to the bag and the load. Tell us what goes inside and we will specify it.

Custom printed paper bags
for your brand
A printed bag leaves the shop with the customer and keeps working: on the street, on a bus, in a kitchen at home. That is why retailers, bakeries and boutiques order their own instead of buying plain stock. We print the everyday kraft bag, laminated luxury bags with rope and ribbon handles, and the block-bottom counter bags that bakeries and delis fill at the counter.
Why buyers choose a made-to-order bag
Stock bags come in fixed sizes, so the product either rattles around or does not fit. A made-to-order bag is built to your dimensions, with the handle and the stock weight matched to what it has to carry, and your logo printed where it will be seen, not stuck on a label.
Materials we print on
Kraft
Unbleached, natural finish. The everyday retail and takeout bag.
Coated paper
Smooth white stock for full-color photographic print.
Laminated
Gloss or matte film over the print, for premium retail bags.
Recycled board
Recycled-content stock where the grade allows it.
Block bottom kraft
Flat-based food stock that stands open on a counter.
Sizes and dimensions
Bags are quoted on width × gusset × height in inches. If you already know the size, send it. If not, tell us about the product: its footprint, its weight and how many go in a bag. We will come back with a size that works and the paper weight to carry it.
Artwork, proofs and repeat orders
Send print-ready artwork or just your logo. Our studio sets the file up, positions it on the die for your size and sends a PDF proof. Nothing is printed until you approve that proof, and the file is kept on record so a repeat order runs without setting it up again.
Discover your custom bags
Tell us what you pack and how many bags you need. One of our team will take you from stock choice to finish, and price the exact job, with no fixed packages and no guesswork.
