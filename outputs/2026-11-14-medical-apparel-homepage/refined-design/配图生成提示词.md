# 精修版配图生成提示词

- 方式：内置 imagegen，未使用 API／CLI。
- 用途：医护服饰工厂首页设计示意；图片不代表用户真实工厂、产品或人员。
- 素材：同目录 assets/，均由本次生成结果原样复制。

## 真人穿着横图局部修订

为避免第一位和第四位男性面貌过于相似，用内置 imagegen 仅修改第四位男性的面貌，其余五人、服装、站位和 3:1 画幅保留。

```text
Edit this photographic lookbook. Keep the exact 3:1 canvas, all six people head to toe, their positions, clothes, colors, shoes, background and lighting. Change ONLY the FOURTH person from the left, who wears slate-blue scrubs: make him a clearly distinct middle-aged East Asian man, clean-shaven, short straight black hair with a little grey at the temples, natural facial texture and a calm friendly expression. His face must look entirely different from the first man in the white lab coat. Preserve realistic anatomy and the original slate-blue scrub garment and standing pose. No text, logos or cropping. All other five people unchanged.
```
